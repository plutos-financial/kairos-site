  • Your savings are always yours

Dollars that stay count for more.

Questions people ask first.

There is more detail on the How it works page.

Prizes are paid only from interest, never from deposits, so the amount you save stays the same whether you win or not. Your savings are in U.S. Treasury bills, not a bank account: Plutos is not a bank and is not FDIC insured. We set out every risk in writing before you deposit.

Each draw uses a public random number from drand. Entries are locked and their fingerprint is published before that number exists, so the result can't be steered and anyone can re-check it. Your chances depend on how much you've saved, how long it was saved that month, and how long it has stayed in: dollars kept in for 3 months or more count up to three times.

The prize pool is the interest that everyone's Treasury bills earn in a month. Plutos keeps 15% of the interest and the rest is paid out as prizes. The more people save, the bigger the prizes.

By bank transfer. You link a bank account in your name, confirm your identity once, and deposit from that account through Stripe; a transfer usually takes a few business days to settle. You can withdraw at any time, back to the same account. Interest on money you take out before the month's draw isn't entered in it.
