Fair, and you can check it

Every part of Plutos can be checked by anyone.

Winners picked by Chainlink

Each draw uses a random number from Chainlink VRF, with a proof that anyone can check. Nobody at Plutos can choose who wins.

Prizes paid from Treasury interest

Savings are held in short-term U.S. Treasury bills. The interest they earn pays for the prizes, so deposits are never used.

Money in and out with Coinbase

Add money with Apple Pay or a debit card, and cash out to your bank account, through Coinbase.

Every transaction is public

Plutos runs on Base. Deposits, draws and prize payments are recorded publicly, so the numbers can always be checked.

More savings, more chances

Your odds grow with what you save. Someone with $1,000 saved has ten times the chances of someone with $100.

Why we're building Plutos

Lottery tickets are popular because a small amount could change your life. But the money spent on them is gone.

Questions

More detail on the How it works page.

Can I lose the money I put in?

Prizes are paid only from interest, never from deposits, so the amount you save stays the same whether you win or not. Plutos is not a bank and won't be FDIC insured, and we'll set out every risk clearly before launch.

How are winners picked?

Each draw uses a random number from Chainlink VRF, which comes with a proof anyone can verify. Your chances depend on how much you've saved and how long it's been saved that month.

How big are the prizes?

The prize pool is the interest that everyone's savings earn in a month. Plutos keeps 15% of the interest and the rest is paid out as prizes. The more people save, the bigger the prizes.

How do I add and withdraw money?

You add money with Apple Pay or a debit card through Coinbase, and you can withdraw your savings at any time. Cash-outs go to the bank account linked in your Coinbase account.
