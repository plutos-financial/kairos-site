Deploy the Plutos contracts to Base Sepolia

No terminal needed. Your wallet signs about 10 transactions: 4 contracts, then 6 setup steps. It costs only free test ETH. The wallet you use becomes the owner and sees the demo controls.

  1. Install MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet in this browser and create a new test wallet (never one with real money).
  2. Get free Base Sepolia ETH from a faucet (links in the README), about 0.01 ETH is plenty.
  3. Click the button and approve each transaction.
